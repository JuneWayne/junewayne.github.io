Actionable Interpretability: Effective Data Curation for LoRA Fine-Tuning SLMs against Prompt Injection

Cisco Foundation AI

Compared eight ways to curate 256 training pairs from the Fine-Grained Indirect Prompt Injection dataset. Designed a Pairwise Selection Score (PSS) to select prompt pairs that linear probes & the model underperforms as training dataset.

Details

First Author · May 2026 – November 2026

Supervisors: Rachel Williamson and Devin Bender

Used a Jacobian Lens to read Qwen2.5-7B’s intermediate hidden states and identify the prompt pairs it found hardest to classify. Used those pairs to fine-tune Qwen with LoRA and examined the feature distribution of the dataset selected by J-Lens.

Evaluated the fine-tuned models on separate prompt-injection benchmarks. The Jacobian Lens yielded the lowest overall attack success rate and selected more cases where the source text falsely claimed the user’s task was complete.

Interpreting Early-Career Job Advertisements with Sparse Autoencoders and Semantic Clustering

Active Learning Lab

Developed PERC (Preprocessing, Embedding, Reduction, Clustering) to analyze 36,081 early-career job postings collected over 1.5 years. Evaluated seven ways to segment a job posting text to identify patterns across occupations and over time.

Details

First Author · January 2024 – November 2026

Supervisors: Dr. Brian Wright and Ali Rivera

Applied Goodfire’s pretrained Sparse Autoencoder (SAE) to examine layer-19 activations from Llama-3.1-8B to identify features associated with technical skills and job responsibilities from occupation and monthly clusters.

Compared hypotheses generated by GPT-6 Luna from SAE features, PERC clusters, and job posting description text alone. Tested the hypotheses on postings from held-out companies and discovered recurring themes about early career job roles.

Rule-Based Attention: Replacing Attention Heads in 3D Generative Mesh Transformers for Efficient Generation

Spatial AI Lab · Ongoing research

Measured which geometric features attention heads attend to during unconditional and conditional generation across four families of autoregressive mesh transformers (MeshGPT, MeshXL, DeepMesh, and MeshMosaic).

Details

First Author

Supervisors: Dr. Lei Li

Designed a preference-ratio metric and three rule-based replacements for attention heads. MeshXL maintained generation quality with up to 50% of the measured heads replaced.

Evaluated conditional and unconditional generation using kernel and Fréchet distances in Uni3D embedding space. Used Chamfer distance to compare generated meshes with their nearest training shapes.

Reward Hacking: Examining the Effects of Weight Deletion on Reward-Hacking Behavior of LLMs

Aikyam Lab · Ongoing research

Investigating whether selectively deleting model weights can make a model more likely to exploit gaps in a reward function, and whether such effects of reward hacking transfers or generalizes to unseen tasks.

Details

Supervisors: Ahson Saiyed and Dr. Chirag Agarwal

Designing controlled experiments to test whether learned weight deletion makes models more prone to reward hacking during coding RL, measuring how often they pass contradictory unit tests and how accurately they solve the original tasks.

Physics-Aware Convolutional Neural Network: A ShearFlow Dataset Adaptation

Visual Intelligence Lab

Trained a physics-aware recurrent convolutional neural network (PARCv2) in PyTorch to predict advection and diffusion in two-dimensional shear flow. Reduced training time by over 67% using one-third spatial down sampling and time-step skipping.

Details

Research Intern · April 2025 – September 2025

Supervisors: Joseph Choi and Dr. Stephen Baek

Reduced rollouts mean squared error by over 81% after comparing Euler and RK4 integrators under the same model configurations and selecting checkpoints by validation loss.

CS 2501: Intro to Research – Multilingual Bias in Gendered Linguistic Context of NLP Models

University of Virginia · Course project · September 2025 – December 2025

An NLP research project that discovered that toxicity and sentiment scores generally changed more for English comments with female pronouns than for those with male pronouns after translation into Turkish or Finnish (the two genderless languages).

Details

Supervisors: Dr. Briana Morrison

Built a four-language dataset by translating a pronoun-balanced sample of RtGender Reddit comments from English into Dutch, Turkish, and Finnish. Used perturbation sensitivity analysis to compare predictions from eight language-specific classifiers.

MAE 6270: Experimental Robotics – Calibrated Automatic & Teleoperated Robotic Navigation

University of Virginia · Course project · September 2026 – November 2026

Designed a four-wheel-drive ROS 2 robot for joystick teleoperation and automated maneuvers. Calibrated its drive system by developing a linear regression model to measure patterns in observed driven distance to expected motion distance.

Details
